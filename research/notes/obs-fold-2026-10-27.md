# iPhone Duo fold and unfold (agent OBS-FOLD, 2026-10-27)

Dirty-room notes behind `specs/simulator/foldable.md`. Prose only. Raw logs,
screenshots, string dumps and the guest probe live in `research/dirty/obs-fold/`
(git-ignored). No disassembly or decompilation. Ghidra, the reference tools
folder and the IPSWs were not opened. Evidence levels as in AGENTS.md rule 4.

## Setup

- Mac15,10, macOS 27.2, Xcode 27.1 at `/Applications/Xcode.app`. Xcode 27 has
  no Simulator.app; simulator windows live in Device Hub
  (`Contents/Applications/DeviceHub.app`, bundle id `com.apple.dt.Devices`).
- One throwaway device, `ycode-test-duo-fold-1` (iPhone Duo, iOS 27.1,
  24A94401), booted, rebooted once, then deleted. No other simulator was
  touched.
- Device Hub: computer-use access was granted, the app was opened in the
  background and its main window was read once (sidebar of devices, toolbar).
  The first click (selecting the test device) was stopped by the owner, so no
  Device Hub control was used and no fold control was seen on screen. That line
  was dropped.
- Instead, a guest-side probe of ours (`research/dirty/obs-fold/foldprobe/`,
  Objective-C, built for `arm64-apple-ios27.0-simulator`, entitlements in
  `__TEXT,__entitlements`) published a client-side HID virtual service in the
  guest and dispatched vendor-defined HID events. Prototypes came from the
  open-source IOHIDFamily headers; the callback-table order was corrected by
  one crash (the table has no leading version field).

## Guest behavior (level 3)

### At boot

- backboardd connects SimulatorHID's 10 services (gamepad, buttons, keyboard,
  proximity, three vendor 0xFF00 services, and **three touch screens**: LCD,
  Resizable, LCD-1, each with its display's UUID). No hinge service among
  them.
- `locationd` starts "Relay service" and creates two client-side virtual HID
  services in the guest: `[DeviceState]` 0xFF0C/0x22 and `[Crane]`
  0xFF0C/0x21, transport Virtual. It immediately dispatches a device-state
  report: orientation portrait, hinge closed.
- `dtuhidd` (started on demand) creates its services, among them
  `avpCustom(0x500)` with usage pair **0xFF61/0x5B** (65377/91), transport
  "CoreDevice".
- SpringBoard's CoreMotion client matches 0xFF0C/0x21 (report interval
  10000) and 0xFF0C/0x22, gets MagicPose `hingeState:Closed`, deviceOrientation
  Portrait, inner orientation Ambiguous. DisplayContentMode resolves to
  `Single`, later `FullyClosed`: cover (UUID of LCD) primary, inner (LCD-1)
  off. SpringBoard sizes: cover 466 × 678 pt, inner 669 × 951 pt.
- Screenshots: LCD shows the home screen; LCD-1 is black. `simctl io
  enumerate`: all display ports power On in every state (no host power change
  was ever seen).

### Injecting the hinge

- Event: vendor-defined, usage page 0xFF61, usage 0x5B, data = the
  IOCFSerialize text of a dictionary `{source, type, value}`. With
  `source=hinge-slider-control`, `type=range`, `value=<degrees>` locationd
  logs "Received AVP event", "Processing AVP event - Source: hinge-slider-control,
  Type: range", "Angle value set to: 90", "All inputs available, computing new
  device state", a device-state report, and dispatches on both of its virtual
  services. backboardd logs "VendorDefinedEvent usagePage:0xFF61 usage:91"
  followed by "Motion event usagePage:0xFF0C usage:34".
- `devicectl device motion hinge-angle` then reported Angle 90, Mech 90.
- IOCFSerialize writes a double as `<integer size="64">`: the value arrives as
  whole degrees.
- locationd hinge state (device-state property B): 1 closed at 0°, 2
  partially open for every tested angle 5–175°, 3 open flat at 180°.
  Property A = device orientation; A1 = inner-surface orientation (0 while
  closed, 1 when open). C = tabletop (always 0 here).

### SpringBoard reaction

- First open, one event 0°→90°: "Hinge state changed from .closed to
  .partiallyOpen", DCM `FullyClosed→FoldingDual`, cover pri→aux, inner
  off→pri, style `.continuousLive`, then the UI interface orientation changed
  from portrait (1) to **landscapeLeft (4)**. Cover screenshot black, inner
  screenshot the home screen in landscape (2853 × 2007 PNG), with widgets
  (Batteries, Screen Time) on page 1 next to Settings, Safari in the dock
  area at the right, search at bottom right. `simctl io enumerate` reported
  "UI Orientation: Landscape Left" for both LCD and LCD-1.
- Sweeps (20 events, 30 ms apart): 0→180 swaps to the inner display when
  SpringBoard's position passes about 90° (logged "progress 0.50,
  toSwap 1.00"), then `.openFlat`; 180→0 swaps back to the cover when the
  position passes about 80°, then `FullyClosed` at 0° (inner aux→off), UI
  orientation landscapeLeft→portrait ("Became primary scene in display content
  mode swap transition").
- 0→40 quick sweep: transition starts, then autocompletes back; stays on the
  cover. 180→100: inner stays primary ("partiallyOpen+foldAvoidance"). 0→100:
  swaps to the inner (as the display tool's help says for "book from closed").
- Slow single steps of 5–10° every ~6 s from 0 to 160: each step starts a
  small transition that autocompletes back; SpringBoard's own hinge state
  stayed `.closed` (although CoreMotion said PartiallyOpen) until 170°.
  SpringBoard's displayed angle differs from the injected one by a few degrees
  (e.g. 120→118.3, 10→14.4).
- Per-display backlight: on unfold BacklightServices moves LCD-1 Off→ActiveOn
  and the cover to off; SpringBoard logs "display power required: NO" for the
  cover. Nothing about this was seen in the host log.

### A running app

- Settings launched while folded: scene on display "Main", bounds 466 × 678.
- Unfold sweep: SpringBoard `[DeviceSceneHandle] … display=Unknown.main[3]
  bounds={{0,0},{669,951}} capability=.Continuous`, interface orientation 4.
  In the app: "Scene did move screens … from <UIScreen A> to <UIScreen B>",
  "Disconnecting screen: <UIScreen A>", orientation mode dynamic→alwaysAll,
  "Scene will change interface orientation: landscapeLeft (4)". The same
  scene session continued (no reconnect). Inner screenshot: Settings in a
  two-column landscape layout.

### Orientation input

- `orientation-picker-control`, type `enum`: `landscape-left` → code 3,
  `landscape-right` 4, `faceup` 5, `facedown` 6, `portrait` 1, `ambiguous` 0
  (no report sent), `portrait-upside-down` rejected ("Unknown orientation").
  MagicPose followed (e.g. deviceOrientation LandscapeLeft). The folded
  home screen stayed portrait (screenshot 1398 × 2034).

### Persistence

- Rebooted while unfolded (180°). After boot: angle 0, cover home screen,
  LCD-1 black. `device.plist` has no fold key; no preference file under
  `data/Library/Preferences` names hinge or fold. Not persisted by the guest.

# Part 2: the `displays` capability and panel surfaces (agent OBS-FOLD2, 2026-10-27)

Answers `foldable.md` §9 Q7 (and parts of Q4). Raw data (capabilities
variants, log streams, screenshots, our two guest probes) live in
`research/dirty/obs-fold2/` (git-ignored). No disassembly, no Ghidra, no
IPSW, no Xcode or Device Hub GUI. Only throwaway devices
`ycode-test-duo-fold2-*` (iPhone Duo, iOS 27.1) were created, booted and
deleted. The Mac was under very heavy load from other work (load average
150–880), so boots took 3–5 minutes and some timing numbers below are
coarse.

## Static facts (level 2)

- The device type's `capabilities.plist` has two top-level keys,
  `capabilities` and `iconState`. `displays` is a key **inside**
  `capabilities` (one of its 89 keys), not a top-level key. Its value is an
  array of five dictionaries, in this order: `LCD` (integrated), `LCD-1`
  (integrated), `TVOut` (tvOut), `Wireless` (carPlay), `Resizable` (scene).
- Key names, value types and values of every entry are tabulated in
  `device-capabilities.md` §3.1. Value types: strings for names, ids,
  colorspace and chrome/mask identifiers; integers for `width`, `height`,
  `screenID`, `powerState`, `surfaceType`, `nativeOrientation`,
  `nativeRotation`; reals for `scale`, `hdpi`, `vdpi`, `refreshRate` and the
  four corner radii; boolean `hasDigitizer`; array of strings
  `requiredFeatures` (Resizable only).
- `surfaceType` exists only on the two `integrated` entries: `LCD` 1,
  `LCD-1` 2. The other three entries have no `surfaceType`, no corner radii,
  no chrome and no mask (Resizable has corner radii 0).
- Other `capabilities` keys that name a display: `ScreenDimensionsCapability`
  (main screen only), `MainScreenClass`, `DeviceCornerRadius`,
  `displayGamut`, `displayport`, `DeviceSupportsSingleDisplayEnhancedMultitasking`
  (false). None of them names a surface or the second panel. `profile.plist`
  has `chromeIdentifier` and `framebufferMask` for the cover only and the
  feature flags `com.apple.display.integrated`, `…supports-external`,
  `…aot`, `com.apple.CoreSimulator.framebuffer.27`.

## How the guest gets it (level 3)

- Every guest process has `SIMULATOR_CAPABILITIES` = the host path of the
  device type's `capabilities.plist` (`ps -E` of SpringBoard; also seen by our
  probe started with `simctl spawn`).
- `SIMCTL_CHILD_SIMULATOR_CAPABILITIES=<path>` on `simctl boot` replaces that
  value in every guest process (SpringBoard's environment showed our path in
  every run). The host side (CoreSimulator's framebuffer and display list,
  `simctl io enumerate`) still comes from the device type: both built-in
  displays, five ports, were published in every run regardless of the file.
- So the panels' roles are a **guest-side** read of this file: SpringBoard's
  surfaces changed with the file while the host's displays did not.
- Which library reads it is not named in any log. Our probe asking
  MobileGestalt for an answer named `displays` got no answer (null), while
  `DeviceCornerRadius` answered 59.0; so `displays` is not served under that
  name by `MGCopyAnswer`, or not to an unentitled client. Not pursued further
  (would need level 4–5).

## Confirmation boots (level 3)

Each boot used a fresh `ycode-test-duo-fold2-<tag>` device and a copy of the
plist changed as listed. "Works" = SpringBoard logs `Main: … surface: .cover`
and `Unknown.main[3]: … surface: .inner`, runs once, reaches
`DCMResolved: Single→FullyClosed`, cover shows the home screen. "Fails" =
both panels `surface: .default`, SpringBoard killed again and again
(launchd `runs` 4–15 within 3–4 minutes), crash reports with
`EXC_BREAKPOINT` in a Swift assertion whose faulting frame is
`ManagedDisplaysController.scene(_:didUpdateIsCloningSource:)` in
SpringBoardDisplay — the same path as Ycode's "Cloning source already
exists" stop. (The assertion text itself is not in the Mac crash report.)

| Variant of `capabilities.displays` | Result |
|---|---|
| unchanged (control: device type's own file, and an identical copy at our path) | works |
| key `displays` removed | **fails** (as under Ycode) |
| `surfaceType` removed from both integrated entries | **fails** |
| `surfaceType` swapped (LCD 2, LCD-1 1) | works with roles swapped: `Main` is `.inner`, `Unknown.main[3]` is `.cover`; FullyClosed makes LCD-1 primary (portrait home screen on the 2007 × 2853 panel) and LCD black |
| only the two integrated entries, all their keys (TVOut, Wireless, Resizable entries dropped) | works |
| two integrated entries without `colorspace` | works |
| two integrated entries without `refreshRate` | works |
| two integrated entries without both `colorspace` and `refreshRate` | works |
| two integrated entries without `chromeIdentifier` | fails |
| two integrated entries without `framebufferMaskIdentifier` | fails |
| two integrated entries without the four `cornerRadius*` | fails |
| entries with only `displayName` + `surfaceType` | fails |
| entries with only `screenID` + `surfaceType` | fails |
| entries with only `displayType` + `surfaceType` | fails |
| entries with `displayType`, `displayName`, `deviceName`, `screenID`, `surfaceType` | fails |
| all keys except chrome, mask, corner radii, colorspace, refreshRate | fails |
| same plus `framebufferMaskIdentifier` | fails |
| same plus the corner radii | fails |

Reading: an integrated entry is honored only when it is complete; one
missing chrome, mask or corner radii is ignored as a whole (its panel falls
back to `.default`). `colorspace` and `refreshRate` are optional; the three
non-integrated entries are not needed for the surfaces. Which of the
fourteen remaining keys (`displayName`, `deviceName`, `displayType`,
`screenID`, `width`, `height`, `scale`, `hdpi`, `vdpi`, `nativeOrientation`,
`nativeRotation`, `powerState`, `hasDigitizer`, `surfaceType`) are each
required, and how an entry is matched to a framebuffer display, was not
bisected further. The swapped run shows that each panel's role is the
`surfaceType` value of its own entry (1 → `.cover`, 2 → `.inner`), not a
rule such as "first integrated display is the cover".

Side findings that correct `foldable.md` §9 Q7 / §10.2:

- On the **reference** too, BacklightServices logs `surfaceType:0` for both
  `LCD` and `LCD-1` at startup, and SpringBoard gives the inner panel a
  ShellSceneKit root of kind `bringupDisplay` (`SSK.primary-1.root(bringupDisplay)`,
  next to `SSK.primary.root(mainDisplay)`). Neither is a symptom; the only
  difference that matters is SpringBoard's `.default` surface.
- In the failing boots the display names were as on the reference (`Main`,
  `Unknown.main[3]`).

## Swap thresholds for Ycode-style sweeps (level 3)

One fresh device with the device type's own capabilities. Our probe (OBS-FOLD's
`foldprobe`, changed to send whole degrees and to run a list of sweeps in one
process) sent each move as **17 events 30 ms apart** ending on the target —
the sweep Ycode's `FoldModel` sends — with 5 s between moves. The panel that
ended up primary was read from SpringBoard's `DCMResolved` lines (first UID =
cover, second = inner, as in the `Single→FullyClosed` line at boot).

| Move | Result (panel primary afterwards) |
|---|---|
| 0 → 60 | cover (a transition to the inner starts, then falls back within ~1.5 s) |
| 0 → 75 | cover (same fall-back) |
| 0 → 85 | **inner** (SpringBoard's own hinge state closed → partiallyOpen) |
| 0 → 90 | **inner** |
| 90 → 180 | inner (openFlat) |
| 180 → 90 | inner (a transition to the cover starts, then falls back) |
| 180 → 85 | inner (same fall-back) |
| 180 → 80 | **cover** (SpringBoard's hinge state partiallyOpen → closed) |
| 180 → 75 | **cover** |
| 85 → 0, 75 → 0, 60 → 0 | cover, `FullyClosed` (inner off) |

So, for this sweep: opening swaps to the inner for a target of 85° or more and
not for 75° or less (boundary in (75°, 85°]); closing swaps to the cover for
80° or less and not for 85° or more (boundary in (80°, 85°]). Half Open (90°)
ends on the inner from both sides. The earlier "about 90° / about 80°"
(OBS-FOLD) were SpringBoard's smoothed positions during long sweeps; these
are commanded targets.

## Q4: backlight of the panel the guest stops using (level 3, partial)

- `simctl io enumerate` keeps every port "Power state: On"; `simctl io poll`
  printed nothing during a fold. The host's view does not change.
- In the guest, backboardd's Backlight category logs "Setting CoreBrightness
  backlight factor:<f> with fade duration:0" (the line does not name the
  display):
  - at boot: factor 1 twice (one per built-in panel), then factor 0 a few
    seconds after SpringBoard resolves `FullyClosed` (inner off);
  - at every swap, in both directions and also for a transition that falls
    back: factor **1** 0.1–1.4 s after the transition's start (the
    incoming panel is lit before it shows), then factor **0** 0.3–4 s later,
    when the transition has settled (the panel that ends up not primary:
    the old one after a swap, the briefly lit one after a fall-back).
  Since the remaining panel keeps showing, the factor is per panel. So the
  guest does switch the outgoing panel's backlight off and the incoming
  one on through backboardd. Whether these reach the framebuffer as
  `SFBDisplaySetBacklightState` (Ycode's framebuffer saw two state-2 calls
  at boot, matching the two factor-1 lines) is to be read under Ycode with
  `YCODE_FRAMEBUFFER_CALLS=1`.
- BacklightServices (in SpringBoard) logs per-panel display modes:
  `DSM:…:LCD prewarmForDisplayMode:Live->Off` when the cover becomes
  auxiliary, `Off->Live` when it is about to become primary again; on the
  inner panel `cbDisplayMode Off→ActiveOn` on unfold (OBS-FOLD).

## Direction of the inner panel's turn (level 3)

- `simctl io screenshot --display=LCD-1` while open: 2853 × 2007, upright
  landscape (widgets on the left, time at top right, Safari at the right
  edge, search at bottom right).
- `simctl io recordVideo --display=LCD-1` at the same moment: the frames are
  stored as **2006 × 2852 portrait** (the framebuffer's orientation; odd
  sizes rounded down by the encoder) with a display matrix of −90° (ffprobe
  "rotation=-90": shown turned 90° clockwise). A frame read without the
  matrix has the Screen Time widget in the lower right half, the upright
  image's left-middle: the stored portrait image is the upright landscape
  image turned 90° **counter-clockwise**.
- So to show the inner panel upright, a host turns the portrait framebuffer
  image **90° clockwise** — Ycode's choice in `foldable.md` §10.1 is right.
  (Mount 270° = the framebuffer's top edge is the upright image's right
  edge.)

# Liquid Glass in Xcode 27.1 (agent OBS-GLASS, 2026-10-27)

Dirty-room notes behind `specs/ide/liquid-glass.md`. Prose and tables only.
Window captures, zooms and pixel profiles are in `research/dirty/obs-glass/`
(git-ignored). No colors and no assets are recorded here: only structure,
material kind, geometry in points and behavior. No disassembly, no
decompilation, no Ghidra. Evidence levels as in AGENTS.md rule 4.

## 0. Setup and method

- Mac with macOS 27.2 (26B5112a), Xcode 27.1 (27A9269) at
  `/Applications/Xcode.app`. The Xcode windows were on the main display
  (3440 × 1440 at scale 1), so one captured pixel is one point.
- System settings, read only (`defaults read`, nothing changed): appearance
  "Auto" (switches with the time of day); Reduce Transparency and Increase
  Contrast not set (off). Neither accessibility setting could be observed
  in its "on" state, because changing them is a Mac setting.
- Sample: `<samples>/ycode-observe-glass/GlassApp`, a
  copy of the audit-6 `AuditApp` (made by the reference's New Project ▸ App)
  with a 423-line `Long.swift` (for scrolling) and a `Many/` folder of 60
  small text files (for a long navigator list). No owner project was opened.
  No simulator was used.
- Control, all in the background (the owner has declined full-screen
  control): Xcode was granted at the click tier. Files were opened with
  `open -g -a Xcode <file>`. Plain clicks (AXPress) on toolbar segments,
  buttons and a disclosure triangle; scroll through the scroll bar's
  accessibility value. Menu presses are refused at the click tier, and so
  are keystrokes, right-clicks, drags and caret clicks in the editor.
- Captures: `screencapture -o -l <window id>` (the window alone, no shadow,
  full resolution); window ids from the window server's window list. Pixel
  rows and columns were profiled to find capsule rims, separators and
  corner curves. Values below are in points from the window's top left.
- "Active" means the window rendered as key in the active app (colored
  window buttons). The background tooling left the reference's project
  window in that state most of the time; the "inactive" state was captured
  twice: while a popover held the key focus, and when the Welcome window was
  key in front of the project window.

## 1. Main workspace window, light appearance, active

Window 1400 × 900; navigator 290 wide (x 0–289), editor x 290–1139,
inspector divider at x 1140, inspector x 1141–1399.

### 1.1 Overall structure

- **No floating sidebars.** The navigator and the inspector run the full
  window height, edge to edge: their background starts at the window's
  left (and right) edge and at its top, under the window buttons and the
  toolbar items. There is no inset, no corner radius and no gap between
  them and the window frame. The navigator/editor boundary has **no divider
  line** (only the change of background); the editor/inspector boundary has
  a 1-point divider line.
- Both side areas have one uniform, opaque-looking background in the
  captures (no wallpaper bleed). The window's own corner is the system's
  continuous corner; the curve covers about 15 points along each edge
  (same in the Welcome window and in Ycode's windows).
- The toolbar has **no bar of its own**: it is 52 points tall, has no
  bottom line and no background separate from what is under it. Over the
  navigator it shows the navigator's background; over the editor it shows
  the editor's background; over the inspector it shows the inspector's.
  Every toolbar item sits in its own floating glass shape.
- Under the toolbar, in the editor column only, the jump bar (y 52–83)
  continues the same background with no line between toolbar and jump bar;
  a separator line (1–2 points, y 84–85) closes the jump bar. Editor text
  starts below it.

### 1.2 Toolbar glass capsules (active)

All toolbar items are glass: a rounded shape with a thin rim, a bright
highlight on the top and bottom inner edge, a slight vertical gradient
inside, and a soft shadow below (visible 1–6 points under the shape). The
fill takes on what is beneath: over the navigator the capsules read like
the navigator, over the editor like the editor. All shapes are **38
points tall**, top at y 7, bottom at y 44 (centered in the 52-point
toolbar: 7 + 38 + 7). Single buttons are **38 × 38 circles**. Multi-part
items share **one capsule**.

| Item (left to right) | Shape | Outer x (rim to rim) | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| Navigators \| Coding Assistant | capsule, 2 segments, no divider while one is selected | 95–172 | 78 × 38 |
| Run \| Stop | capsule, divider line between | 208–282 | 75 × 38 |
| Go Back \| Go Forward | capsule, divider line between | 297–371 | 75 × 38 |
| Activity view (icon, name, status) | **no shape**: plain icon and text | — | — |
| New Conversation | circle | 569–606 | 38 × 38 |
| Scheme › Destination | capsule | 613–803 | 191 × 38 |
| Issues ("⚠ 1") | capsule | 810–872 | 63 × 38 |
| Xcode Cloud notices | circle | 879–916 | 38 × 38 |
| Add Editor \| Editor Options | capsule, no divider | 923–1002 | 80 × 38 |
| Canvas \| Assistant \| Code Review | capsule, divider before the third | 1009–1133 | 125 × 38 |
| Hide/Show Inspector | circle, over the inspector | 1355–1392 | 38 × 38 |

- **Spacing:** 6 points of clear background between neighboring shapes
  (803 → 810, 872 → 879, …); 7 points from an area edge: Run|Stop ends 7
  points before the navigator's right edge, Back|Forward starts 7 points
  after it, the editor-mode capsule ends 6 points before the inspector
  divider, the inspector button ends 7 points before the window's right
  edge. The first capsule starts 15 points after the window buttons
  (window buttons at x 18–79).
- **Navigator hidden:** the editor reaches the window's left edge, the
  window buttons sit over the editor background, and the left items close
  up with the same 6-point gaps: 95–172, 179–253, 260–334. The two-segment
  Navigators capsule now shows a divider line (no segment selected).
- **Inspector hidden:** the inspector button keeps its place (7 points from
  the right edge) and now ends the right group with an 11-point gap after
  the editor-mode capsule; the right group otherwise keeps its 6-point
  gaps.
- **Selected segment:** a recessed pill inside the capsule (for example the
  selected Navigators segment: about 32 × 26 points, fully rounded, about
  4–5 points inside the rim). The accessibility frame of a segment is
  36–37 × 35 (pass 6); the capsule adds about 1.5 points of rim on each
  side.
- The accessibility frames of single toolbar buttons are 39 × 51 (the
  whole toolbar height); the visible circle is 38 × 38.

### 1.3 Navigator (left area)

- **Navigator selector bar** (the nine navigator icons): one glass capsule
  **278 × 30** at x 6–283, y 51–80 (6 points from both side edges of the
  area, directly under the toolbar). Shadow below it, no divider line under
  it. The selected icon has a recessed pill (about 30 × 24).
- **Bottom bar**: a 31 × 30 glass circle "+" (x 6–36) and a glass capsule
  filter field **242 × 30** (x 42–283), y 863–892, 6 points apart, 7 points
  above the window's bottom edge. The filter field's own glyphs (filter
  menu, recent-files and source-control toggles) sit inside the capsule.
- **The outline scrolls under both glass bars.** With a long list scrolled
  to the middle (observed with the window inactive), rows are fully legible
  right up to the capsules' edges
  (no fade above or below them); rows passing under a capsule are seen
  through it, blurred and dimmed; rows continue below the filter bar to the
  window's bottom edge. The overlay scroller runs from below the selector
  bar to above the filter bar. There is no solid header or footer strip:
  the bars float over the list.
- **Selection highlight**: a rounded rectangle 22 points tall, inset 10
  points from both sides of the area (x 10–279), corners about 5 points;
  in the active window with focus elsewhere it is a neutral (not accent)
  fill.

### 1.4 Coding Assistant pane (left area, the second segment)

- The same left area; its header is two glass capsules, "New Conversation
  ⌄" (x 7–157) and "New Group" (x 164–282), 30 points tall at y 51–80,
  7 points apart and 7 points from the edges: the same metrics as the
  navigator selector bar. No divider under them.

### 1.5 Editor area

- **Top:** jump bar 32 points (y 52–83) plus a separator line, sharing the
  toolbar's background. The editor's scroll view is **not** visible under
  the jump bar: scrolling the editor changes nothing in the jump bar band.
  In the toolbar band above it, a very faint, heavily blurred trace of the
  scrolled-away text appears (only a few percent of contrast, shaped like
  the text lines and the minimap below) once the editor is scrolled away
  from the top, and nothing appears while it is at the top. Reading: the
  editor reports a soft top scroll-edge effect to the window, but the
  jump bar is an opaque band; the effect is all but invisible in practice.
- **Bottom (debug bar):** the editor's bottom bar is 36 points tall
  (y 864–899 with the debug area hidden) with a separator line above it.
  Editor text is clipped at that line, but the bar itself shows the text
  that scrolls beneath it **strongly blurred** (colored smudges of the
  text). Reading: a bottom bar with a **hard** scroll-edge style (dividing
  line plus blurred, mostly obscured content).
- With the debug area shown, the same bar sits at y 763–798 between two
  separator lines (above it the editor, below it the debug area); it still
  shows the editor text blurred beneath it.
- The minimap and the vertical overlay scroller are not glass.

### 1.6 Debug area

- Variables view (left) and console (right) split by a 1-point line. No
  glass: their bottom bars carry borderless glyph buttons, the scope pop-up
  ("Auto ⌃") and **flat filled search fields** 22 points tall (y 871–892),
  corners about 6 points, no rim, no shadow. No separator line above these
  bottom bars.

### 1.7 Inspector (right area)

- **Inspector tab bar**: one glass capsule **248 × 30** at x 1146–1393,
  y 51–80 (5 points from the divider, 6 from the window's right edge),
  with the selected tab as a recessed pill (about 78 × 24, about one third
  of the capsule).
- Inspector content is **not glass**: section headers in plain text;
  text fields and pop-ups are **flat filled rounded rectangles** (about 24
  points tall, corners about 6, no rim); the Target Membership list is a
  bordered box with a small "+ − ✎" button strip at its bottom.

### 1.8 Popovers

- Xcode Cloud notices popover (from the toolbar circle): a **translucent,
  frosted** panel (the content beneath tints it; a capture that lacks the
  backdrop renders it nearly black), **360 points wide**, about 108 tall,
  corners about 17 points (continuous), **with an arrow** about 10 points
  tall and 26 wide at its base, whose tip touches the button's bottom
  (2 points below it). No visible rim. Its buttons ("Dismiss",
  "Get Started…") are flat filled rounded rectangles; a circular help
  button "?".
- New Conversation popover (from the toolbar circle): the same material and
  arrow, 376 × 176 window; a multi-line prompt area with a placeholder, a
  separator line above a bottom row (attach and options glyphs at the left,
  a circular send button at the right).
- Opening a popover made it the key window: the project window drew in
  its inactive state while the popover was open (§2).

### 1.9 Sheets

- "Choose Targets" (Target Membership "+"): the sheet is **centered on the
  parent window in both directions** (432 × 266 at x 484–915, y 317–582 in
  a 1400 × 900 window) — not attached under the toolbar. Opaque, corners
  about 17 points, no visible rim. The whole parent window, toolbar
  included, is **dimmed uniformly** (about a fifth darker) while the sheet
  is up. Buttons 75 × 24 (accessibility 73 × 24), flat filled, default
  button accent-filled. Its accessibility role is AXSheet.

### 1.10 Welcome window (480 × 632)

- Opaque window background, no title bar or window buttons; the same
  continuous window corner.
- Close button: a **glass circle 38 × 38** at x 19–57, y 19–57 (rim,
  highlight and shadow like the toolbar circles; accessibility frame
  36 × 36 at (20, 20)).
- Open…, Clone…, New Project ⌄: **flat filled capsules** (no rim, no
  highlight), 35 points tall drawn (accessibility 36), widths 107/107/130.
  Not glass.
- Recent list: a filled rounded box x 60–420 (360 wide), y 295–572, corners
  about 12 points, no border line; the selected row is an accent-filled
  rounded rectangle inset 8 points (x 68–412), 48 points tall, corners about
  8; separator lines between rows start under the text, not under the icon.

### 1.11 Find navigator header

- The Find navigator's header (the "Find › Text › Containing" path, the
  find field, the scope pop-up) sits on the area background under the
  selector capsule and ends with a separator line. The find field is a
  rounded rectangle about 22 points tall with corners about 8 (not a
  capsule, not glass).

### 1.12 Full screen (owner-arranged)

- Midway, the owner put the project window into full screen on the
  built-in display (1512 × 982 points). The window server then lists the
  content window at 1512 × 949 under the 33-point menu bar and the
  **toolbar as a separate, full-width window 1512 × 52** at the same top
  edge, laid over the content window. The content keeps its 52-point top
  band (the navigator selector capsule and the jump bar sit at the same
  offsets as in a normal window); in the content window's own image that
  band is empty, because the toolbar lives in the other window.
- The full-screen window has square corners (no window corner curve).
- Display-scope screenshots of that display were interrupted (twice), and
  the window was off-screen for the window server, so the toolbar window
  itself could not be captured; the content window was read through the
  background capture tool only. The navigator's list again showed through
  the bottom glass capsule.

## 2. Inactive window (light)

Seen with a popover key and with the Welcome window key.

- **Glass flattens.** Every toolbar capsule, the navigator selector bar,
  the inspector tab bar and the bottom glass bars lose their highlight,
  gradient and shadow: each becomes one flat fill (slightly darker than the
  area behind it) with a plain outline. Shapes, sizes and positions do not
  change.
- Side areas: the navigator and inspector backgrounds become slightly
  lighter. The editor and the toolbar band over it do not change.
- Window buttons turn neutral; toolbar glyphs and texts dim; the selection
  highlight in the navigator becomes lighter.
- Content under glass is still seen blurred through the bottom filter
  capsule in the inactive state.

## 3. Dark appearance

Not observed. The appearance is "Auto"; it stayed light from 17:08 until
18:50 local time (sunset about 18:30), checked every 10–30 seconds with
`defaults read` and with a probe window of ours. Presumably the automatic
switch waits until the Mac is idle, and the owner was working. Changing the
appearance is a Mac setting; relaunching the reference with a per-process
appearance would need quitting it (a menu press, refused).

## 4. Ycode today (for the gap list)

- `YcodeApp` debug build from this branch (worktree `obs-glass` at
  7c7e47d), wrapped in a scratch bundle with its own identifier.
- **The binary declares SDK 15.0.** `LC_BUILD_VERSION` of the SwiftPM-built
  `YcodeApp` reads minos 15.0, **sdk 15.0**, although the link step uses
  the macOS 27.0 SDK (a plain `swiftc` link against the same SDK records
  sdk 27.0). AppKit keeps its pre-26 appearance for binaries that declare
  an older SDK, so Ycode cannot get any Liquid Glass look until the
  shipped binary declares SDK 26 or later. This matches what the Welcome
  window shows: old-style push buttons with a bezel and drop shadow, a flat
  close button without a rim, a bordered recent box.
- **Probe of the mechanism** (our own 20-line AppKit program, one window
  with a push button and a two-segment control, linked twice against the
  macOS 27.0 SDK): declared sdk 27.0 → flat filled push button and
  segmented control, white window background, the macOS 26+ look; declared
  sdk 15.0 (the same object file, linker told 15.0) → bezeled white push
  button with a shadow, gray window background, the pre-26 look. So the
  declared SDK version alone switches the design, on this OS.
- **Opening a project crashes the debug build** at launch: a main-actor
  isolation check fails in the document controller's
  `maximumRecentDocumentCount` getter, which AppKit calls from a
  background operation queue while noting a recent document. So the
  workspace window could not be observed running; the gaps below for it
  come from source names (`ycode/Sources/YcodeUI`).
- Source names: the window uses `toolbarStyle = .unified`; the split view
  uses `NSSplitViewItem(sidebarWithViewController:)` and
  `NSSplitViewItem(inspectorWithViewController:)`; toolbar groups are
  `NSSegmentedControl`s in custom-view items (no `NSToolbarItemGroup`); no
  `NSGlassEffectView`, no `NSButton.BezelStyle.glass`, no
  `NSScrollEdgeEffectStyle`, no split-view-item accessories, no
  `NSBackgroundExtensionView`. Several panels draw their own background
  (`PanelBackgroundView`). Welcome buttons use `.flexiblePush`/`.push`,
  the recent box draws its own corner radius (10).

## 5. Not observed, and why

- Window **active vs. inactive** is covered only as far as §2: the
  inactive state was reached indirectly; hover and pressed states need the
  pointer over the window (display-scope control).
- **Menus** (menu bar menus, context menus, pop-up and pull-down menus),
  **Settings**, **Open Quickly**, the **Library**, the **editor tab bar**
  and **find bar**: each needs a menu press or a keystroke, refused at the
  click tier. (Opening a second file with `open -g` replaced the editor's
  document; no tab bar appeared.)
- **Window resizing** needs a drag or a menu press. **Full screen** was seen
  only partly (§1.12): the toolbar window could not be captured.
- **Dark appearance**: §3.
- **Reduce Transparency / Increase Contrast** on: both are off and are Mac
  settings.
- **Focus rings** in fields: need keyboard focus in the reference.

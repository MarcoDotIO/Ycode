# Council round 1: how the values were measured (2026-11-04)

Dirty-room note. It records the method behind the "Council round 1
measurements (2026-11-04)" sections in `specs/ide/liquid-glass.md`,
`liquid-glass-ycode.md`, `metrics.md`, `editor-area.md` and
`navigators.md`, and behind the checklist `specs/ide/fidelity-council.md`.
It contains no code. The script is
`research/tools/council-r1/measure_captures.py`, which only reads the
captures.

## Inputs

- The session `research/dirty/captures/2026-11-04-council-r1/` from the
  capture worktree (git-ignored, read-only here). It holds `xcode/` and
  `ycode/`, each with a manifest, 1x PNG stills before and after each step,
  ProRes recordings, and JSON sidecars that give each frame's
  presentation time and wall-clock time.
- The judges' material in `research/dirty/council/r1/`: the brief, three
  reports, the comparison sheets and `timing-summary.json`.
- The test project's own `project.pbxproj` (one synchronized root
  folder) and the repository's `.editorconfig`, which the reference found
  in an ancestor folder.

## Conditions that shape every number

1. **1x.** Stills and recordings are 1400 × 880 pixels for a 1400 × 880
   point window, so one pixel is one point. Advances, glyph heights and
   corner radii carry ±0.5 pt of rounding. The font size could not be told
   from 13 vs 13.3 pt, because both round to an 8-point advance at 1x.
2. **Inactive windows.** The session runs with the apps in the
   background. The window server reported both windows as on screen, but
   the window buttons are neutral grey, the toolbar shapes are flat fills
   with outlines, and the titles are dimmed. Every colour is therefore an
   inactive-state value.
3. **sRGB.** Stills are sRGB PNGs. Recordings are 12-bit 4:4:4 ProRes,
   converted to 8-bit RGB by ffmpeg. Colours were taken from stills only;
   recordings were used for positions and timing.
4. **Variable frame rate.** The recorder writes frames at 17–23 ms
   intervals (median about 19 ms). Times come from each frame's sidecar
   time, not from the frame index.
5. **Synthetic input.** Scrolls were posted as discrete events at a fixed
   pace with no momentum phase. "Open" was a Launch Services open of the
   file (`open -a`), not a click. The inspector toggle was an
   accessibility press.
6. **The Ycode in the session declared SDK 15.0.** The capture wrapper
   bundles the bare debug executable. Its `LC_BUILD_VERSION` (read with
   `otool -l`) says minos 15.0, sdk 15.0, so AppKit drew Ycode's
   pre-macOS-26 fallback. Most of the judges' "no glass" findings come
   from this.

## Methods

**Colour runs.** Along a chosen row or column of a still, consecutive
pixels within 2–4 levels of each other were merged into runs, each with
its median colour. Edges, bands, separators, fills and outlines were read
from these runs: the jump bar, the separators, the selection bands, the
minimap shade, the scroll-bar tracks, the inspector's fields and rules,
and the toolbar's capsule fills.

**Glyph ink boxes.** For a box around a known glyph, the pixels more than
40 levels from the box's median were taken as ink, and their bounding box
measured cap height, x-height, baselines and the right edges of line
numbers. Character advance came from the left ink edges of glyphs at known
columns of the generated file. On line 1 the glyphs of columns 0 to 58 sit
on an 8.0-point grid (cells start at 341 + 8n) with no drift, and the same
grid fits lines 3, 17, 18 and 30. Line pitch came from the baselines of lines 1 and 3 (36
apart, two rows) and was checked across wrapped lines.

**Token colours.** For each token, the five pixels farthest from the
background were taken; their median is the full-coverage colour. Each
token kind gave the same value on several lines to within one level,
which shows those pixels were fully covered. Icon colours were taken the
same way, ranked by saturation instead of distance.

**Corner radii.** The coverage of each pixel in a corner patch was
computed from its colour between the background and the fill. Those
values were compared with the coverage of ideal circular corners,
supersampled 16 × 16, and the best radius kept. The navigator selection
fits 8 and the current-line band 4. A continuous corner would fit a
slightly larger circle; to confirm at 2x.

**Toolbar shapes.** On row 26 (mid-height), pixels darker than the same
column's toolbar background (row 2) mark the outlines and fills, and
spans 30 points or wider are the shapes. Their tops and bottoms came from
column runs. This was repeated on stills 01, 02 and 08 to see the centre
group move.

**Scroll travel.** The rows of the content area in the after-still were
matched against the before-still over shifts of ±900 points, by least
mean absolute difference. The best match was exact: a shift of 600 in
the reference and 566 in Ycode.

**Scroll-under strength.** For every frame of the editor scroll
recording, the mean darkness (255 minus the grey level) was computed in
regions inside each bar, chosen away from the bar's own text: the toolbar
band above and below its capsules, the jump bar to the right of its path,
the sticky header to the right of its text, and the bottom bar. The same
was done for the open text area as a reference. The bars' median darkness
over the scroll, divided by the text area's, gives the share of content
that shows through, about 6 %. Per-pixel maxima show the veil has no
shapes. In the navigator, the before and after stills of the navigator
scroll were differenced inside the selector capsule, the filter capsule,
the toolbar band, and the gap under the selector.

**Timelines.** Each frame was differenced against the one before (any
channel changing by more than 8 levels) and the changed pixels counted
per region: navigator rows, toolbar title, jump bar, editor, inspector.
Times were taken relative to the action time in the manifest. This gave
the open-document sequence (+567, +812 and +1164 ms) and showed that the
navigator expansion happens in a single frame.

**Inspector slide.** On row 500 of each frame, the inspector's 1-point
divider was found as a column darker than both neighbours two columns
away. On row 300, the image's left edge was found as the first non-white
pixel. The divider gives the slide (260 points); the image edge gives the
editor's re-centring (130 points). The header text stays 8 points right of
the divider in every frame, so the inspector moves rigidly. Progress is
the divider's travel over 260. A cubic Bezier was fitted by grid search
over the start and end times (5 ms steps) and the four control values
(0.05 steps), by least squares. Named curves were fitted with only their
start and end free.

**Input versus motion.** The manifests give each action's start and end
in wall-clock time, and the sidecars give each frame's. Comparing them
showed that both apps' scrolls start in the first frame after the first
event and stop within one frame of the last. The judges' duration
differences equal the difference in how long the harness took to deliver
the same input to each app.

**Minimap scale.** Bar pitch (3 points per visual line, against 18 in the
editor) and the length of a 78-column bar (104 points, against 624 in the
editor) both give 1/6. The shade's height (124) is the editor's visible
height (757) divided by 6.

## Corrections to the judges

- The judges' minimap widths (80, 115, 140) were estimates. The minimap
  measures 127 plus a 1-point separator. With the two widths in
  `reference-ui-5.md` §6.5, the data fit min(128, 15 % of the editor's
  width).
- Navigator rows are 22 and indentation is 14 per level (not 20, 16 or
  24).
- Toolbar shapes are 38 tall (not 28–32 or 34–36). The toolbar is 52 in
  both apps (not 44 in Ycode).
- Gemini's syntax colours do not match the captures; Sol's keyword, type
  and comment colours do. Sol's "number" colour is the declaration-name
  colour.
- The inspector motion lasts about 215 ms (not 310), and the inspector
  travels 260 (not 205). The 310 ms window included the pressed-state
  frame and a trailing toolbar frame. The 205 came from whole-frame phase
  correlation mixing two motions.
- The editor scroll moved 600 in the reference (not 589). The navigator
  and editor "slower in Ycode" findings come from the harness.
- Xcode's Text Settings are not all disabled. Only the indentation
  controls are, because indentation comes from the repository's
  `.editorconfig`. Encoding and line endings stay enabled, with grey
  "No Explicit" titles.

## Limits

No value in the specs depends on Ghidra, disassembly or Apple's files:
everything is black-box (level 3), read from screen captures of the
running reference. The specs copy no images, icons or artwork from the
captures. Icons are described by colour family only.

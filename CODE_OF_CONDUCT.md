# Code of Conduct

Ycode is a project people work on together, so we want it to be welcoming and respectful for everyone,
whatever their background, identity or level of experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and take criticism about the work, not the person.
- Say so when you are wrong, and help others learn from it.
- Think about what is best for the people who use and build the project.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discrimination, or jokes at the expense of a person or group.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

This code applies in the project's spaces (issues, pull requests, reviews and any other forum the project runs),
and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/MarcoDotIO), or
[report the content to GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Reports are handled as privately as possible. Nobody will be penalized for a report made in good faith.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone who
breaks this code, in proportion to what happened.
